Logistics

Phase 1 — ground roads & trucks
Tools
Industry
Storage tier for the next Mine/Mill/Town. Can't be resized later in Phase 1. A Mine always makes Ore; a Mill always turns Ore into Steel.
Network
Ground/Elevated applies to Road, Ramp, Connect, One-Way and Demolish. Uncheck auto-connect to lay road tiles side by side without joining them — handy for parallel one-way streets — then use Connect / Disconnect to wire up (or sever) specific edges by hand. Facing sets the single side of the next Station that can ever touch a road; a Mill needs two Stations — one handling Ore for delivery, one handling Steel for pickup.
Vehicles
A Bulk Truck can only ever be given Ore-handling Stations as stops; a Flatbed can only be given Steel-handling ones. The order editor enforces this — a mismatched stop is rejected.
Rail
A Rail Depot has real storage, like a Mill — it's what lets a train drop off dozens of units at once while trucks draw it down a few at a time. Build a Station touching it for road access, same as a Mine or Mill; track touching any side gives it rail access, no facing needed.
A train isn't bought off the shelf — it's assembled at a Train Yard from one Engine (its physics: speed, mass, engine/brake force) and however many identical Wagons you pick (its cargo: capacity, and the one resource that Wagon type carries — same fixed-per-vehicle rule as a truck). Pick engine/wagon/count, then click a track tile touching a Yard to assemble and pay for the train there.
About
Trucks load and unload at Stations, never at a Mine, Mill, or Town directly. A Station must touch a Mine, Mill, Town, or another Station — chain Stations together to reach a road from an industry that isn't right next to one. An elevated road never connects to a ground road at the same cell — only a Ramp links the two layers. Trains work the same way but on their own network — track instead of road, Rail Depots instead of Stations. A signal is rail's one-way: it also marks a hard block boundary, so two trains can never occupy the same signaled segment at once.
